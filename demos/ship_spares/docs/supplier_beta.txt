Supplier Terms for Beta Ocean Traders

**Supplier Terms Document**

**1. Payment Terms:**
Payment is due within 30 days of the invoice date. All payments must be made in USD. Late payments will incur a 5% interest charge per month until full payment is received. For orders over USD 5000, payment terms are extended to 60 days.

**2. Delivery Times:**
Standard delivery times are 10 business days from the date of order confirmation. For urgent deliveries, a 20% surcharge will be applied to the order value. Urgent deliveries are expected within 5 business days.

**3. Warranty Period:**
All spare parts sold by Beta Ocean Traders are covered by a 12-month warranty from the date of delivery. Warranty coverage includes defects in materials and workmanship. Parts that are damaged due to misuse, improper installation, or other user-related issues are not covered under the warranty.

**4. Minimum Order Value:**
The minimum order value for Beta Ocean Traders is USD 500. Orders below this value will be charged at a minimum of USD 500.

**5. Return Policy:**
Returns are accepted within 30 days of delivery, provided the product is in its original packaging and unused. A 20% restocking fee will be applied to returned items. Shipping costs for returns are non-refundable. Returns must be approved by Beta Ocean Traders before shipping.

Beta Ocean Traders reserves the right to terminate this agreement at any time. Any termination will be effective 30 days after written notice is provided. All outstanding orders will be processed and paid for.
