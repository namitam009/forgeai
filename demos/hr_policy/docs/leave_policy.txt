Shipping companies often have specific needs for shore staff who are responsible for managing operations and maintaining equipment. Here's a realistic company HR Leave Policy for a shipping company's shore staff:

---

Shipping Company Name
HR Leave Policy

**Annual Leave:**
- Employees are entitled to 20 days of annual leave per year, pro-rated for partial years.
- Annual leave can be carried forward for a maximum of 10 days per year, subject to approval.

**Sick Leave:**
- Employees are entitled to 10 days of sick leave per year.
- Employees must provide a doctor's note for each sick leave day.
- Sick leave cannot be carried forward.

**Maternity/Paternity Leave:**
- Maternity leave: 12 weeks for the birth of a child, with 2 weeks of pre-birth leave and 2 weeks of post-birth leave.
- Paternity leave: 14 days for the birth of a child, with 7 days of pre-birth leave and 7 days of post-birth leave.
- Leave is pro-rated for partial weeks.

**Application Process:**
- Employees must submit a leave request form at least 14 days in advance for annual leave and sick leave.
- For maternity/paternity leave, employees must provide a doctor's note within 7 days of the expected delivery date or birth.
- Approval for leave is granted by the Human Resources Department.
- Employees must return to work within the leave period unless otherwise approved by the company.

**Policy Effective Date:**
- This policy is effective from January 1, 2023.

---

This policy ensures that shore staff can manage their work-life balance while maintaining operational continuity.
