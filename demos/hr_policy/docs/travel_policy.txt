Business Travel and Expense Policy

1. Purpose: This policy is designed to ensure that all business travel and related expenses are managed efficiently and transparently. It aims to promote accountability and compliance with all relevant laws and regulations.

2. Scope: This policy applies to all employees who are required to travel on behalf of the company for business purposes.

3. Flight Class: Business class is the only approved class of travel for all business trips. Economy class is not allowed for any business travel. Business class tickets will be reimbursed at 100% of the cost.

4. Hotel Limits: The maximum amount per night for hotel expenses is Rs. 2000. Any expenses exceeding this amount must be pre-approved by the department head and the Vice President of Operations.

5. Daily Food Allowance: The daily food allowance for business travel is Rs. 500. This allowance is to be used for meals during business travel and must be documented. Any additional expenses for meals must be pre-approved by the department head and the Vice President of Operations.

6. Claim Deadline: All travel and expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the end of the trip. Failure to submit claims within this period may result in delays in reimbursement.

7. Approval Needed: All travel and expense claims must be approved by the department head and the Vice President of Operations before reimbursement. Any claims that do not meet the requirements of this policy may be denied.

8. Additional Notes: 
- All travel must be booked through the company's designated travel agency.
- Employees are responsible for ensuring that all travel and expense claims are accurate and complete.
- Any discrepancies or irregularities in travel and expense claims will be investigated and appropriate action will be taken.
- This policy is subject to change and will be reviewed annually.
