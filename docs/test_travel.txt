Travel Policy
Employees may book economy class for flights shorter than 6 hours.
Business class is allowed only for flights longer than 6 hours and needs director approval.
Hotel stays are reimbursed up to 8000 rupees per night in metro cities and 5000 rupees elsewhere.
Daily food allowance during travel is 1500 rupees.
All travel claims must be submitted within 15 days of returning, with original bills attached.
Cab expenses are reimbursed only when public transport is not available.
Employees must get written approval from their manager before booking any travel.
